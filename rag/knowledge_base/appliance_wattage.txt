Typical residential appliance wattages in India: a 1.5 ton split air conditioner draws approximately 1400-1600W; a double-door frost-free refrigerator is rated around 150-250W though its actual average draw is much lower due to compressor cycling; a 32-43 inch LED television uses approximately 60-120W; a top-load washing machine draws approximately 400-600W during a wash cycle; a storage water heater (geyser) draws approximately 1500-2000W; a ceiling fan draws approximately 60-75W; and an LED bulb draws approximately 7-12W.

These are rated or typical values for estimation purposes only. Actual wattage varies by brand, capacity, and appliance age, and should be treated as an approximation rather than a precise meter reading.
