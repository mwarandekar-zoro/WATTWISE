LED bulbs typically use 75-85% less energy than incandescent bulbs and roughly 25-30% less than CFLs for the same brightness, while lasting significantly longer.

A 9W LED bulb produces roughly the same light output as a 60W incandescent bulb, so replacing old bulbs is usually one of the fastest-payback changes in a household.

LEDs produce very little heat compared to incandescent bulbs, which slightly reduces the load on air conditioning in the same room as a secondary benefit.

Unlike CFLs, LEDs reach full brightness instantly and are not affected by frequent on-off switching, making them well suited to rooms with short, frequent use such as bathrooms or hallways.
